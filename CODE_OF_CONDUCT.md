# Code of Conduct

mythings is a small, friendly project. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) to feel welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds and opinions.
- Keep discussions on topic and help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone from the project.

To report a problem, email the maintainer at dutta@dynocoder.com. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere. It is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
